# Pilot review

Inspect the prepared image, frozen source context and both actual outputs. Copy review.csv per reviewer so independent assessments remain separate. Scores are blank until a person has reviewed them.

Rate visual factuality, contextual relevance, usefulness (a sighted review is only a proxy), and clarity/concision from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong). For unsupported_claims record a count, with each unsupported material claim and its evidence in review_notes. Separate clearly attributed caption facts from visually supported facts. Record missing essential information, unreadable labels and identity guesses. Include failures; do not silently remove them. This small development pilot is not a user study or held-out statistical comparison.
